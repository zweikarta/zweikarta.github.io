Why GeldSpace

Most people own things in many places: a broker, a savings account, some gold, a flat. Each app shows one slice. GeldSpace brings it together, so you always know what you have and how it's growing.

Two spaces. You decide what goes where.

Everything you add lives in Mine or in Ours. You can move the items you added between them at any time.

Mine

  • Only you can see it.
  • Your partner never sees your Mine: not the names, not the values, not even how many things you have.
  • Your own total includes Ours too, tagged so you can tell them apart.

Ours

  • Shared wealth you both see and update.
  • You can see who changed what, and when.
  • Nothing appears here unless one of you puts it there.

No partner? GeldSpace works fully on its own. Ours appears once you invite someone.

How it works

  1. Add what you own

    Pick a type, give it a name and a value. Gold and crypto get a suggested value from today's price. Your first asset takes under a minute, and you don't need an account to start.

  2. Check in once a month

    A gentle reminder arrives on the day you choose. Last month's values are already filled in, so unchanged items take one tap. About two minutes, each.

  3. See how Ours grew, together

    When you've both checked in, you get a short recap of Ours and a few conversation prompts for a calm money date. Your couple streak pauses on a missed month, and never resets.

What to expect

What GeldSpace does

  • Shows what you own, what you owe, and the difference.
  • Tracks how it changes month by month.
  • Handles stocks and funds, gold, bonds, cash, property, crypto and loans.
  • Works in your main currency, which you can change.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't connect to your accounts. You enter every number yourself.
  • It doesn't give financial advice.
  • It doesn't track spending or set budgets.
  • It has no ads, and we never sell your data.

Questions

Can my partner see my total?

No. Your partner sees Ours only, plus whether you've finished your monthly check-in. Your Mine is never added to anything they see.

Do I need a partner to use it?

No. GeldSpace is complete on your own. Ours only appears once you invite someone.

Where is my data stored?

Your account data is stored in data centres in the European Union. You can export everything as a spreadsheet, or delete everything, at any time from Settings. The privacy policy has the details.

What happens if we stop sharing?

Each of you keeps your own history. The Ours items you added become yours, and the ones your partner added go back to them. GeldSpace shows you exactly what goes where before you confirm.

How do I sign in?

With Apple or Google. You can add your first asset without an account, and sign in when you're ready to keep it safe.

What does it cost?

GeldSpace is free to download, with no in-app purchases.
